AP English Language – Argumentative Essay Rubric (Total: 100 points)

1. Thesis & Argument (30 points)
   - Excellent (26-30): Defensible, specific thesis; every body paragraph advances it.
   - Good (20-25): Clear thesis, but development is uneven.
   - Developing (12-19): Thesis is vague or merely descriptive.
   - Insufficient (0-11): No identifiable thesis.

2. Evidence & Commentary (25 points)
   - Evidence is relevant, sufficient, and explicitly connected back to the thesis.

3. Organization (20 points)
   - Logical progression, coherent paragraphs, effective transitions.

4. Clarity & Style (15 points)
   - Precise sentences, appropriate academic register.

5. Grammar & Mechanics (10 points)
   - Spelling, punctuation, and grammar do not impede meaning.
