Phones in School

Phones are a big topic these days. Everyone has one and everyone uses them all the time.
In this essay I will talk about phones in school and some things about them.

First of all phones can be bad. Some people say they distract students. I read somewhere
that phones are distracting. This is true because when you get a notification you look at
it. Teachers get mad about this and I understand why they get mad.

But also phones can be good sometimes. You can look stuff up. My friend used his phone for
a project once and it worked out fine. Also if there is an emergency your parents can call you
which is important.

Another thing is that some kids dont have computers at home. So the phone is like their
computer. That is another reason.

In conclusion phones have good things and bad things about them. Schools should think about
it carefully and make a good decision. Everyone has different opinions and thats okay. In my
opinion its complicated.
